# Acer Nitro 5 Hardware Failure Diagnosis

## Overview

Hands-on hardware troubleshooting project involving an Acer Nitro 5 AN515-55 that unexpectedly shut down and subsequently showed no signs of power.

## Problem

The Acer Nitro 5 suddenly powered off while gaming.

After the shutdown:

- No charging LED
- No power LED
- No fan activity
- No keyboard response
- No display output
- Power button produced no response

## Troubleshooting Process

### Initial Inspection

Opened the laptop and inspected:

- Motherboard
- Battery compartment
- Battery connector
- RAM
- Cooling system
- Internal cables
- Visible signs of electrical or thermal damage

The motherboard did not show obvious widespread burn damage during visual inspection.

### Battery Investigation

Abnormal residue/discoloration was discovered around the battery compartment.

The original battery was removed and kept disconnected.

A replacement battery was subsequently evaluated, but abnormal leakage was observed and it was also removed from the laptop.

Both batteries were kept disconnected while troubleshooting continued.

### AC Power Test

With the battery disconnected, the laptop was connected to the appropriate AC adapter.

The laptop still showed:

- No charging LED
- No power LED
- No fan activity
- No display
- No response to the power button

This narrowed the investigation toward the laptop's AC power input and motherboard power circuitry.

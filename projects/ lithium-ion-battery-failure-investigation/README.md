# Lithium-Ion Laptop Battery Failure Investigation

## Overview

Investigation into abnormal battery behavior discovered during troubleshooting of an Acer Nitro 5 AN515-55.

## Situation

During inspection of the Acer Nitro 5, abnormal residue and discoloration were discovered around the internal battery compartment.

The original battery was removed from the laptop.

A replacement battery was later evaluated, but abnormal leakage was also observed.

## Investigation

### Original Battery

- Removed from the laptop.
- Inspected the surrounding battery compartment.
- Checked nearby motherboard components for visible damage.
- Documented residue/discoloration around the battery area.
- Kept the battery disconnected.

### Replacement Battery

- Connected for evaluation.
- Abnormal leakage was observed.
- The battery was immediately removed.
- The battery was not used for further testing.

## Safety Considerations

Potentially damaged lithium-ion batteries should not be repeatedly connected, charged, punctured, bent, or otherwise manipulated.

Both batteries were kept disconnected while the laptop diagnosis continued.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Hardware safety
- Battery inspection
- Laptop disassembly
- Risk assessment
- Visual fault identification
- Technical documentation

## Status

**Investigation Complete**

The Acer Nitro 5 AN515-55 experienced a catastrophic electrical failure on the motherboard's battery power circuit. A near-zero-resistance reading was measured across the battery power rail with the system unpowered, confirming a hard short circuit. Physical evidence around the battery compartment, the sudden shutdown under load, and the complete lack of power afterward support the diagnosis. The exact failed motherboard component was not isolated.
